02The offers

Four offers, one per segment, mutually exclusive. Every customer sees exactly one. Percentage off carries the coffee subscription. Complimentary gear is reserved for the annual commitment, where the bigger incentive is paid for by twelve locked months.

Core offer233 coffee repeaters

1 · Subscribe & Save 15

Your usual coffee, on autopilot. 15% off every order, delivered monthly. Skip, swap, or cancel anytime.

Why it works: they already reorder every ~30 days by hand. This removes the chore and pays them for it. 15% is the subscribe-and-save standard, low enough to protect margin, high enough to beat "I'll just order when I remember." The anytime-skip line kills commitment fear.

2 · Annual Roast Club

Prepay 12 months and get 20% off the whole year plus a free Ceramic Pour-Over Dripper (€24) or Double-Wall Glass Mugs (€22) in the first box.

Why it works: these 80 have ordered 4+ times and bought recently. They are not deciding whether they like the coffee, they are deciding how to pay for it. Gear as the gift beats deeper discounting: it costs ~€12 landed, feels like €24, and a dripper on their counter is a daily brand ad. Prepay moves a year of cash to day one.

3 · Explorer Upgrade

Swap your single bag for the Roaster's Variety Box (3 × 250g) subscription at 10% off: €32.40/mo, plus a free pack of paper filters in box one.

Why it works: 90 people buy Huila, Yirgacheffe, or the variety box. They shop for novelty, and novelty is the #1 reason coffee subs get cancelled ("bored of the same bag"). A rotating head-roaster pick solves boredom AND doubles the ticket, from ~€15 to €32.40. The smaller 10% discount holds because the value story is curation, not price.

4 · Wake the Sleepers

"We roasted your usual." 25% off your first 3 months back, then it settles at the standard 15% subscriber rate.

Why it works: these 78 rebuilt the habit 3+ times, then went quiet. A third of the audience is already leaking. Reactivating a lapsed known buyer is far cheaper than acquiring a stranger, and the steeper front-loaded discount is temporary by design: it decays to the same 15% everyone else gets, so it never trains permanent discount-hunting.

04Shipping it in Recharge

Everything above maps to standard Recharge + Shopify primitives. No custom code.

1 · Segment the audienceTag customers in Shopify from the repeat-buyer analysis: upsell-hot, upsell-core, upsell-sleeper, upsell-explorer. One tag per customer, in that priority order.
2 · Create the discountsFour Recharge discount codes: NORDKAP15 (15% recurring), ROASTCLUB20 (20% on the annual plan), EXPLORER10 (10% recurring on the Variety Box), WELCOMEBACK25 (25%, first 3 charges).
3 · Add the annual planThe 8 coffees already carry a monthly Recharge plan. Add a 12-month prepaid plan for Roast Club and attach the free gear item to the first shipment.
4 · Send the emailsOne Klaviyo flow per segment, referencing the exact product each customer rebuys ("your Colombia Huila, on autopilot"). Personal beats clever: they should feel seen, not marketed to.
Assumptions and sources. Audience built from 14 months of Recharge Demo Coffee order history: customers with no active subscription who bought the same product 2+ times in a row roughly 30 days apart (241 today; 135 similar customers were converted to real subscriptions earlier in this demo). Average coffee item €15.38. Gear cost assumed at 50% of retail. Take rates are planning scenarios, not forecasts; subscribe-and-save conversions on a warmed audience commonly land in the 20 to 40% range. Year-1 revenue assumes converts stay subscribed 12 months; any churn under ~5%/mo still beats the do-nothing decay curve. All figures EUR, aggregate demo data only.
