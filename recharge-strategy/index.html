The Six Plays

Each card is a buildable action. Copy Build Prompt puts a ready-to-run Claude Code prompt on your clipboard — paste it into a session with the Recharge MCP + Shopify CLI connected. Click the status pill to track progress (saved in this browser).

Play 01 · Foundation

Tag & Segment the Candidates

Audience: 190 remaining strong + 61 moderate

Tag every candidate subscription-candidate (plus lapsed/active) in Shopify and build a customer segment. Everything else targets this segment.

Why: no offer can ship until the audience exists as a targetable object.
Play 02 · Core Offer

Subscribe & Save 15%

Audience: 190 remaining monthly re-buyers

Monthly selling plan at 15% off on all 8 coffees, pitched per-customer on the exact product they already re-buy. Their cadence is ~30 days — zero behavior change.

Why: House Blend €12.50 → €10.60. They're manually doing this every month; remove the work.
Play 03 · Sweetener

Free Filters on First Order

Cost: €6.50 per conversion

Free Paper Filters 100-Pack with the first subscription order, then offered as a recurring add-on every other month.

Why: #1 attach item in the cohort — and it seeds a second recurring line on the same subscription.
Play 04 · Retention

Month-3 Gear Reward

Audience: new subscribers, renewal 3

Stay subscribed 3 months → free Glass Mugs (€22) or 50% off the Grinder or Kettle. A locked reward that turns trial subscribers into retained ones.

Why: the cohort buys gear alongside coffee, but only ~7% each have pulled the trigger.
Play 05 · Upsell

Variety Box Swap

Audience: 26 Variety Box re-buyers + all subscribers

"Swap any month for the Roaster's Variety Box (€36)" — subscriptions are already swappable in Recharge; make it a feature, not a support ticket.

Why: raises average order value ~2.7× on swap months for explorers.
Play 06 · Winback

Winback the Lapsed 84

Audience: 84 lapsed monthly buyers

Same subscribe & save offer with a stronger hook: 20–25% off the first subscription order on the coffee they used to buy monthly.

Why: the habit is proven — they drifted, they weren't lost. Cheapest re-acquisition you'll ever run.
