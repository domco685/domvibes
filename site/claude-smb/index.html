THE BASICS (AKA WHERE'S COWORK?)

First thing I went looking for was "Cowork." Plot twist: it's not a separate place anymore. Its powers are just part of Claude now. You install plugins and run everything from the normal chat box.

⌨️ SKILLS

Type / in the message box and you'll see your skills. A skill is a packaged recipe for one job. You can call one by name, or just describe what you want and Claude picks the right one.

🔌 CONNECTORS

Connectors are your actual apps: Shopify, Canva, Meta Ads, Gmail and so on. They're what let Claude do things in your store instead of just talking about them.

🎁 PLUGINS

A plugin is a bundle of skills that know how to use your connectors together. Install one and its skills show up in your / list.

THE BIG ONE

THE SMALL BUSINESS PLUGIN

The plugin I used is Anthropic's Small Business plugin. Install it from the plugin directory, then say "help me get set up." It walks you through connecting your first tools and learns about your business.

I wasn't sure at first whether it worked with Shopify, because the setup guide only lists QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva, Gmail and Docusign as defaults. It does. Shopify is one of its newer integrations, and it plugs into the briefings, marketing, restocking and month-end workflows.

The connectors I ended up running alongside it:

SHOPIFYCANVAMETA ADSHIGGSFIELDADWHISPRVERCEL

👉 Running more than one store? Start every request with which store you mean. I have several, and Claude can switch between them, but only if you tell it which one.

THE SECRET SAUCE

RUNS AT 7AM

THE DAILY TREND-TO-CAMPAIGN ROUTINE

This is my favorite thing I built. Every morning Claude scans the internet for trends, news, weather and viral moments, matches them to my products, and has a ready-to-launch campaign waiting before I'm out of bed. I set it up for mindprint, my AI-designed apparel store.

  1. Check the store. Pull yesterday's and last week's sales by product from Shopify.
  2. Scan for trends. Use web search for news, holidays, weather and events, Higgsfield for trending TikTok sounds and formats, and AdWhispr for the competitor ads that have run longest.
  3. Match. Pick the three best trend-to-product matches, score each on fit, urgency and effort, and flag the winner.
  4. Build drafts. An unpublished Shopify landing collection, an inactive discount code, Canva graphics, a short video concept, and a paused Meta ad.
  5. Brief me. A one-page brief plus a phone notification. I say yes, and it goes live.
daily-routine.txt
Set up a daily scheduled task for my [STORE NAME] Shopify store. Run every day at 7:00am [TIMEZONE] with a phone push notification when done.

WHAT THE STORE IS: [what you sell, price range, best-known products, and whether it's print-on-demand or stocked]. Goal: [e.g. grow fall sales].

EACH RUN:
1. Store check (Shopify): yesterday's and last 7 days' sales by product; note top sellers and products with views but no sales.
2. Trend scan:
   - Web search: today's news, viral moments/memes, upcoming dates and holidays, weather, big events relevant to my products.
   - Higgsfield: this week's trending TikTok sounds and viral video formats.
   - AdWhispr: brands in my space actively running ads now; surface their longest-running ads.
3. Match: pick the 3 best trend-to-product matches, each tied to specific products. Score each on fit, urgency, effort; flag the #1.
4. Build the #1 idea as DRAFTS ONLY:
   - Shopify: an unpublished themed collection to use as a landing page, plus an inactive discount code.
   - Canva: social graphics sized for feed and stories.
   - Higgsfield: a short video concept set to a trending sound; run the virality predictor on it.
   - Meta ad: copy, targeting and creative, created PAUSED with no spend.
5. Report: a one-page brief with today's moments, the 3 ideas, links to every draft, and a "say yes to launch" list.

GUARDRAILS: Never publish, activate, post or spend money without my explicit approval. Skip tragedies, disasters and divisive politics. No trademarked characters, logos or celebrity likenesses. Don't repeat an idea used in the last 14 days.

THE TUTORIAL ARCADE

6 CARTRIDGES

SIX PROMPTS. SIX THREADS.

Give each of these its own chat so the context stays clean. Fill in the [yellow bits], paste, and go.

1

HOW TO CONNECTGet your tools talking to each other.

Do this first. Claude checks what's already hooked up, tells you what's missing for running an online store, and tests every connection.

connect.txt
Help me get my tools connected for my Shopify business(es): [store names].
1. Check which connectors are live right now and confirm which Shopify store is active and how to switch between my stores.
2. Tell me what's missing for running an online store (email, calendar, accounting, Canva, Meta Ads, TikTok, Higgsfield, AdWhispr) and which ones matter most for me.
3. For each missing one, show me how to connect it.
4. Finish with a one-line test of each connector so I know it works.
Keep it plain-English and one step at a time.
2

NEW STORE ON SHOPIFYFrom "I have an idea" to a store you can preview.

Claude suggests names, checks domains, and generates real Shopify store previews you can click through.

new-store.txt
I want to start a new Shopify store. Idea: [what you'd sell, who it's for, the vibe].
1. Suggest 10 business names, then check domain availability and price for my top 3 picks.
2. Generate store previews with themes and starter products so I can compare looks.
3. Recommend a starter catalog (5–10 products), pricing and a sourcing approach (print-on-demand, wholesale or dropship).
4. Give me a launch checklist: payments, shipping, policies, collections, a discount code and launch posts.
Don't sign me up for anything or spend money without asking.
3

BRIEFINGS & ROUTINESYour business, summarized before coffee.

Scheduled tasks that run whether or not you're at your computer, and ping your phone when they're done.

briefings.txt
Set up recurring briefings for my stores. Store(s): [store(s)].
- Daily at 7am [timezone]: yesterday's sales, orders needing attention, low stock and one thing to do today.
- Monday: a weekly brief (sales trend, top/bottom products, the calendar for the week).
- Monday: a growth brief (what's working in marketing, competitor moves, three actions).
Send a phone alert when each one is ready. Keep each brief to one page.
Before creating anything, show me the schedule and contents for approval, and don't duplicate any scheduled tasks I already have.
4

MANAGE INVENTORYKnow what to reorder before you run out.

Works out how fast each product is selling, predicts when it runs out, drafts the reorders, and finds the stock that's just sitting there.

inventory.txt
Store: [store]. Help me manage inventory.
1. Pull current stock and sales history, then calculate sales speed per product (last 7 and 28 days).
2. Predict when each item runs out and flag anything at risk in the next 30 days.
3. Size reorder quantities for 60 days and draft purchase orders and supplier emails, but don't send them.
4. List slow movers and dead stock, with ideas to clear them (bundles, discounts, promos).
Skip reorder math for print-on-demand items and just report what's selling.
5

MAKING THEME CHANGESRedesign without breaking your live store.

The golden rule: always work on a copy of your theme. This prompt builds that rule in.

theme.txt
Store: [store]. I want to change my theme: [describe the change, e.g. new homepage banner, featured collection, announcement bar, colors, a new section].
Rules:
- Always work on a COPY of my live theme, never the published one.
- Show me what you'll change before doing it, then give me a preview link.
- Don't publish until I say "publish."
- Tell me how to roll back if I don't like it.
If something can't be done through the connector, tell me exactly where to click in Shopify admin instead.
6

MARKETING CAMPAIGNSResearch, landing page, creative and ads in one go.

A full campaign, built and parked in draft. You check it over, then flip it on.

campaign.txt
Store: [store]. Build a marketing campaign for: [product/collection + goal, e.g. "fall jackets, 50 sales by Nov 15"]. Budget: [$ amount].
1. Research: current trends, competitor ads that have run longest, and the best angle for my products.
2. Shopify: a campaign landing collection (unpublished) plus a discount code (inactive).
3. Creative: Canva graphics for feed and stories, plus a short video concept with a trending sound (Higgsfield) and a virality score.
4. Meta ads: campaign, ad set and ads built in my Meta Ads account, all PAUSED, with targeting and copy explained.
5. A launch plan: posting schedule, email copy and what to measure.
Nothing goes live, publishes or spends money until I approve.

HOUSE RULES

✋ DRAFTS FIRST

Every prompt says "nothing goes live without my yes." Keep that line in.

🏪 NAME THE STORE

Got more than one shop? Say which one at the top of every thread.

🧪 COPY THE THEME

Never edit your live theme. Duplicate it, preview it, then publish.

💸 PAUSED ADS ONLY

Ads get built paused. You switch them on, never the robot.

🔐 GUARD THE KEYS

Only connect your store to tools and people you actually trust.

🚫 NO TRAGEDY-JACKING

Trend-hunting skips disasters, divisive politics and other people's logos.
