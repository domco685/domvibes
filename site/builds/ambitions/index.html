AMBITIONS: FROM A TEXT THREAD TO A REAL PRODUCT

I'm a builder. Building has never been more fun or more powerful, and honestly I'm hooked. But that superpower has a shadow: it's never been easier to get lost in ten projects across ten tools. 01 · THE IDEA

DIVERGENT THINKERS DON'T NEED MORE IDEAS

It started as a spec for a coach that lives in iMessage. You text it what you want, it texts you back one thing to do today, and it keeps a record of proof that you're actually moving. No app to open. Just the thread.

The core loop never changed: pick your chief aim, become the person who does it, do one thing a day, log the evidence. It pulls from Napoleon Hill's chief aim and Neville Goddard's "assume the feeling," made practical and plain.

The brand mark is Shisharka, which means pinecone, an old symbol of creative source. The company behind it is TINKERTOWN LLC.

ambitions / the-loop.md
➜ dump every ambition you have ➜ find your chief aim ➜ who are you becoming? # identity first ➜ today's ONE thing # become + do ➜ log the evidence # proof it's working ✓ repeat daily. channel the energy.

06 · LESSONS

WHAT I LEARNED

  • Doing is cheap now. Deciding is the scarce thing.Agents can build almost anything. The hard part is choosing what's worth building. Which is exactly the problem AMBITIONS solves, so I'm building it for me first.
  • One room, one decider.Seven agents can argue a headline in circles. The headline went back and forth between four of them in one day. It stops when the human picks. Lock the copy and move on.
  • PRs plus screenshots = I can run this from my phone.Every change shows up as a pull request with QA shots. I read, I say go, it ships. I never have to open a laptop to know what changed.
  • Never fall back to fake data.An early build quietly showed a demo persona's candle shop whenever a call failed. I'm the first real user, so I'd have seen someone else's plan as mine. Fail loud instead.
  • Ship the free thing first.A free Ambition Map from 3 questions beats "download an app and start a trial." The map is the product. The app and the connector are two ways to use it.
07 · WHAT'S NEXT

GET IT ALL OUT, THEN FIND THE THREAD

Right now we're exploring a new onboarding. It's a branch to test, not a change of direction. Instead of asking for one ambition, it lets you dump everything you want to build, work or personal, big or small. The more you get out, the clearer your true purpose gets.

brain dump→what · why · limits→threads→what pulls you→chief aim

Then AMBITIONS shows you how your ambitions tie together and suggests your chief aim. You keep it or swap it. Nothing gets thrown away. Every other ambition stays on your map.
